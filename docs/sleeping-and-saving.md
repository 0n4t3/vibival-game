# Sleeping and saving

[Guide home](README.md)

## Sleeping

After 12 minutes awake you are *Tired* (shown under the clock) and can sleep
anywhere. Press **Z** twice, or open the inventory (Tab) and use **Sleep
here** under *Rest*. The Rest section also tells you how safe your spot
feels. Eight minutes pass while you sleep, and your energy and water drain a
little.

### Where you sleep matters

| Makes it riskier | Makes it safer |
|---|---|
| Swamp water within 10 m: an amphibian attack is nearly certain. Past 30 m there is none. | A lit campfire close by |
| Carrying raw meat, bleeding, or a carcass or guts pile nearby (predators smell it) | Rocky high ground |
| Sleeping near water: insect bites and leeches | Tree cover (trunks and boulders around you) |
| Rain: chills, and floods on low ground that wash away loose items | |

Inside a finished house (or out on its porch) nothing attacks, bites or
clings to you in the night, whatever the weather or the water nearby.

If something attacks during the night, you wake partway through it. Fight it
off or get away, and survive 30 seconds.

### The bedroll

A **Bedroll** is woven by hand from 8 string (8 steps) once you have a
spindle. It is for a finished house: right-click (Use) to set it down on
the floor of the room, not on the porch and not right beside the fireplace.
Look at it and:

- press **Z** to lie down at once (no second press), when you are tired and
  nothing stops you sleeping. Asleep on a bedroll, you heal half as fast
  again as sleeping rough: blood, sickness, venom and wounds.
- press **E** to save the game without sleeping, unless something is hunting
  you.
- right-click to roll it up and put it back in your bag.

If the house is torn down, the bedroll is left on the ground.

## Saving

Vibival has two kinds of save, and each world keeps its own.

### The camp save

The game saves **every time you sleep**, a daytime nap as well as a night's
sleep (after an attack wakes you, once you have survived 30 seconds). A
[bedroll](#the-bedroll) also saves without sleeping. When you die you can
**wake at your last camp**, back where you last slept.

### Save and quit

From the pause menu (Esc), **Save and quit to title** or **Save and quit
game**. Closing the window does the same. This leaves a one-time save where
you stand. **Continue** resumes from it once and then uses it up. Your camp
save is kept, so dying later still sends you back to your last camp.

You can't save and quit while something has you in its jaws or is hunting
you, while you sleep, or when you are dead. **Quit to title without saving**
leaves your saves as they were.

## Worlds

Every world has a name, its own map, and its own camp and quit saves.

- **New world**: name it, then start in the lab tutorial or go straight to
  the swamp.
- **Continue**: resumes the world you played last that has a save.
- **Load world**: lists every world with its day and when you last played
  it. **Load** continues it. A world with nothing saved yet shows **Start**,
  which begins it again from the beginning. **Delete** asks you to press it a
  second time to confirm.

A save from before named worlds existed shows up as a world called
**My world**.

## Debug worlds

Tick **Debug world** when you make a new world. It is for testing, and for
players who just want to explore. Debug worlds are marked "[debug]" in Load
world. In one:

- The pause menu has **Save now (debug)**, which overwrites the camp save
  with this exact moment. **F5** does the same while you play.
- Save and quit works at any time, even with something hunting you or holding
  you in its jaws.
- You can sleep at any time, however rested you are or whatever is nearby.
- **Double-tap jump** to fly, and again to land. Hold jump to rise, Ctrl to
  sink and Shift to go faster.
- **F1** (or **Debug panel** in the pause menu) opens the debug panel, which
  pauses the game. It can:
  - set the day and the time of day
  - start rain or any rare weather event, or clear the weather
  - give you any item
  - spawn any creature 5 m in front of you (amphibians settle as they would
    there: buried or lurking in water, basking or roaming on land)
  - pick the **next fish** to bite on any line (never a nibble), or Random
  - switch damage, needs (hunger, thirst and stamina) and hostile animals
    off and on; these switches are saved with the world
  - sleep now, save now, or heal and feed you fully

The only limit is death: you can't save once you are dead.
