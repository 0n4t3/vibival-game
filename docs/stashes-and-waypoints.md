# Stashes and waypoints

[Guide home](README.md)

## Stashes

A sack does two jobs. There are two kinds:

| Sack | Made from | On your shoulder | Set down |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Woven Frond Sack** | 4 cordage, 8 plant fiber (by hand) | carry 25 kg (17 more) | a stash of up to **50 kg** |
| **Woven Cloth Sack** | 8 string (by hand, once you have a spindle: see [Crafting](crafting.md#string-and-the-bow-drill)) | carry 48 kg (40 more) | a stash of up to **100 kg**, paler and bigger |

- **On your shoulder** means anywhere in your inventory. Only your **best**
  sack counts: carrying a second one doesn't add to it, it only weighs you
  down.
- **Set down**, it becomes a stash. Picked up again, it is the same sack.

To set it down, put the sack in your hand (select its hotbar slot) and
right-click. It lands a step in front of you, on firm ground. It won't go down
in the water.

Remember that without a sack on your shoulder you can carry only 8 kg again
(or what your other sack allows). Weave a second sack if you want both.

### Using a stash

Look at it and press **E**. Your inventory opens beside the sack's contents.

- **Click** an item, then click a slot on the other side to move it there.
  Dropping it on a different item swaps the two if both sides can take the
  weight.
- **Right-click** an item to move the whole stack across in one go.
- **Take all** moves everything you can carry out of the sack.
- **Store all but hotbar** moves everything except your hotbar into the sack.
- **Pick up sack** takes the sack back once it is empty.

Food in a stash still spoils. Stashes are saved with the world, both in the
camp save and when you save and quit. If you die and wake at camp, stashes
are back as they were when the camp save was made.

Walk more than a few metres away and the stash closes.

## Waypoints

A simple placeholder until proper navigation arrives. You can have one
waypoint at a time.

- Press **M** to put the waypoint where you are looking, up to 250 m away.
  Looking at the sky marks the spot where you stand.
- An amber beam rises from the spot, and an amber diamond on screen shows
  where it is and how far away, in metres. When it is off to the side or
  behind you, the diamond waits at the edge of the screen in its direction.
- Press **M** again within 6 m of the waypoint to clear it. Anywhere else, M
  moves it.

The waypoint is saved with the world.

A good habit: before you set out to gather or explore, stand at camp and look
down at your feet, then press M. The waypoint will lead you home.
