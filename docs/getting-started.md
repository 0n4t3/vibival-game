# Getting started

[Guide home](README.md)

## The story

You are running a particle accelerator experiment in a lab. Something goes
wrong in a flash of light, and you wake in a hot, heavy-aired swamp full of
giant ferns and scale trees: the Late Carboniferous, 307 million years ago.
You have nothing. Stay alive.

You come to on your back, staring up at the sky. A huge dragonfly drones over
you. Then lightning strikes a tree close by, which crashes down, and its stump
is left burning. That fire is your first: take an ember from it before it
dies (see [Fire](crafting.md#fire-and-cooking)).

## Starting a world

From the title screen choose **New world**, give it a name, and pick a start:

- **Start in the lab (tutorial)**: ride the elevator down to the
  breakroom, have two coffees (pick up the mug, pour at the coffee pot,
  then drink it with right-click or Use), and read the papers on the table
  to find a keycard. Then pick up parts in the lab, craft and install the
  accelerator components, and switch it on. This teaches crafting and the
  inventory. The pages are worth reading: they hint at things you will
  need later.
- **Start in the swamp (skip tutorial)**: go straight to the swamp.

Tick **Debug world** first for a world where you can sleep and save at any
time, fly, and use the F1 debug panel. See
[Sleeping and saving](sleeping-and-saving.md#debug-worlds).

Each world has its own map and its own saves. **Continue** resumes the world
you played last. **Load world** lists every world so you can load or delete
it. See [Sleeping and saving](sleeping-and-saving.md).

## Your first hour

1. **Gather.** Pick up sticks and stones (E). Strip ferns for plant fiber and
   fiddleheads: the ones you can strip are bigger and brighter than the dark
   ferns everywhere, with curled fiddleheads standing up in the middle and
   pale, stringy old fronds round the foot. Look for dark chert nodules on rocky ground, and for river
   stones and chert washed up along the shore (carefully: see step 5).
2. **Make a campfire** and light it with an **ember** from the burning stump
   (it burns for a day and glows for three more). Set the ember in the
   campfire (E) and blow on it (E) within three hours, before it goes out.
   Press C to kneel and craft on the ground: lay the parts out from the tray
   and do the work by hand. The recipe book is under Tab. Later, at a
   workbench, a clay **spindle** spins fiber into string, and string and two
   sticks make a **bow drill**, which lights a fire anywhere.
3. **Knap a chert blade.** Press C to kneel, lay a chert nodule on the
   ground, choose the blade and strike flakes off it (you need a river stone).
   A blade butchers kills fully and cuts more fiber. Then make an axe or a
   spear.
4. **Find food.** Search rotting logs for grubs, gather seed fern seeds, and
   pick fiddleheads. Cook everything on the fire.
5. **Get water carefully.** Dig clay from a clay bank, make a pot, fire it in
   the campfire, fill it at the water's edge and boil it. Read
   [Creatures](creatures.md) before you go near water.
6. **Weave a sack** (4 cordage and 8 fiber) to carry 25 kg instead of 8.
7. **Sleep** once you are tired (after 12 minutes awake), far from the water
   and beside a lit fire. Sleeping through a night saves the game.

### Touch screens

On a phone or tablet the controls are on the screen. Using a gamepad or a
keyboard instead? Switch them off with **Settings → Touch controls**.

- **Walk**: put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side of the screen
  and slide it. Let go to stop.
- **Look**: drag anywhere else.
- **Right side**: Hit, Use, Jump, Act (pick up, drink, harvest, open a
  stash), Run and Drop. Run stays on until you stop walking.
- **Top right**: Pause (II), Bag (inventory, health and recipes), Craft,
  Mark (waypoint) and Sleep (tap twice).
- **Hotbar**: tap a slot to hold that item.
- **Menus**: tap to click. Select an item to eat, drink or treat wounds
  with the buttons beside it. Back (top right), or your phone's back button,
  closes the menu.

## The screen

- **Bottom left**: the stamina bar; above it how hungry and thirsty you are
  ("Mildly Hungry", "Thirsty"... nothing while you are fine: see
  [Hunger and thirst](survival.md#hunger-and-thirst)); and above those a couple
  of words saying how you are ("Healthy", "Bleeding badly", "Broken leg,
  feverish"...). There is no health bar: see [Survival](survival.md#health).
- **Bottom centre**: the hotbar, the item in your hand, and the weight you
  carry against your limit.
- **Top right**: the day and the time of day. It says *Tired* when you can
  sleep.
- **Centre**: the crosshair and the prompt for whatever you are looking at.
- **Waypoint**: an amber diamond with the distance in metres, held at the edge
  of the screen when it is off to the side or behind you.
